Hao Wei Tu 杜浩瑋

I’m Hao Wei Tu, a communication designer and researcher working between Melbourne and Taipei.我是杜浩瑋，在墨爾本與台北從事傳達設計與研究。

Design practice設計實務

My practice spans identities, books and exhibitions for commercial and cultural clients. At D&D Creative in Melbourne, I work as a contract Design Director on projects including the rebrands of Midcity Centre and Downtown Grocer.我的設計涵蓋商業與文化領域的品牌識別、書籍與展覽。目前以合約制設計總監身分參與墨爾本 D&D Creative 的專案，包括 Midcity Centre 與 Downtown Grocer 的品牌重塑。

Research研究

I’m a PhD candidate in Design at Swinburne University of Technology, working with Design Factory Melbourne and ANSTO. My research explores how generated images can support communication and collaboration in science.我目前在斯威本科技大學攻讀設計博士，與墨爾本設計工廠及 ANSTO 合作，研究生成圖像如何支持科學領域的溝通與協作。

Full biography完整簡歷

Hao Wei Tu is a communication designer and researcher — a PhD candidate in Design at Swinburne University of Technology, working in collaboration with Design Factory Melbourne and the Australian Nuclear Science and Technology Organisation (ANSTO) — and a Design Director (Contract) at Melbourne studio D&D Creative, where he has led major rebrand projects across the CBD including Midcity Centre and Downtown Grocer. He specialises in brand strategy, visual storytelling and identity systems for both commercial and cultural clients, balancing aesthetics with strategic communication.杜浩瑋是一位傳達設計師暨研究者，斯威本科技大學設計博士候選人，與墨爾本設計工廠（SDFM）及澳洲核能科學技術組織（ANSTO）合作進行研究。同時擔任墨爾本設計工作室 D&D Creative 設計總監（合約制），主導墨爾本市中心多項品牌重塑專案，包含 Midcity Centre 與 Downtown Grocer。專精於商業與文化機構的品牌策略、視覺敘事與識別系統，於美學與策略性溝通之間取得平衡。

In 2022, Hao Wei made history as the first designer from Taiwan and East Asia to receive The One Club's Young Ones Portfolio Award in New York. His work has earned multiple international honours, including six Red Dot Design Awards, a Gold at Design for Asia, Silver, Bronze and Merit at The One Club ADC, the AIGA 365 Design Awards, shortlist recognition at D&AD, and Excellence Awards across Communication Arts. His work has been featured multiple times in Communication Arts Magazine and exhibited internationally in Taiwan, Macau, Germany, Japan, and Poland.2022 年，浩瑋創下歷史，成為台灣與東亞首位獲得紐約 The One Club 青年作品集大獎（Young Ones Portfolio）的設計師。作品先後榮獲六座德國紅點傳達設計獎、香港 DFA 亞洲最具影響力設計金獎、紐約 The One Club ADC 銀、銅與 Merit 多項殊榮、美國 AIGA 365 設計獎、英國 D&AD 入選，以及《Communication Arts》多項 Excellence Awards。作品曾多次刊載於《Communication Arts》雜誌，並於台灣、澳門、德國、日本、波蘭等地展出。

More recently, his portfolio has been recognised as a Winner at the New York Type Directors Club's TDC 72 (2026), at the Taiwan International Graphic Design Awards (2025), shortlisted in Communication Arts Design (2025), and selected for the Golden Bee Global Biennale.近期，作品入選紐約 Type Directors Club TDC 72（2026）、2025 年台灣國際平面設計獎，於 Communication Arts Design（2025）入圍，並入選莫斯科金蜂海報雙年展。

In 2025, he served as a mentor for the AGDA Professional Mentorship Program, was a finalist for Emerging Designer of the Year at the AGDA Awards, and spoke at TEDxSwinburne. He was a juror for the Design Institute of Australia's Graduate of the Year Awards in both 2025 and 2026. In 2026, he was invited to mentor Master's students through the Sint Lucas Antwerp – Karel de Grote-Hogeschool Internship Program.2025 年，浩瑋擔任澳洲設計師協會（AGDA）專業導師計畫導師，入圍 AGDA Awards 年度新銳設計師，並於 TEDxSwinburne 擔任講者。他於 2025 與 2026 年擔任澳洲設計協會（DIA）Graduate of the Year Awards 評審。2026 年受邀擔任比利時 Sint Lucas Antwerp – Karel de Grote-Hogeschool 碩士生實習導師。
