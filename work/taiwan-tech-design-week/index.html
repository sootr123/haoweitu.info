Taiwan Tech Design Week臺科大設計週

Event identity, 2019活動識別，2019

Taiwan Tech Design Week is an inter-school design carnival encompassing exhibitions, workshops, performances, forums, talks and portfolio reviews. The identity uses the door as a metaphor for uncertainty, possibility and the desire to discover what exists on the other side. “Knock knock” becomes the central verbal and visual device: we try, touch and knock, then wait for a response.

A geometric door form establishes the basic structure of the system, while a white polygon marks the point at which the door is struck. Radiating shapes and bold fields of colour visualise the sound and energy produced by the knock.

Because Design Week encompasses many different activities, the identity was designed to be systematic yet flexible. The same core elements can be reorganised into varied compositions across invitations, badges, exhibition graphics, Behance Reviews and workshop materials while maintaining a consistent visual language.

臺科大設計週是一場跨校的設計嘉年華，包含展覽、工作坊、表演、論壇、講座與作品集評論。識別以「門」比喻未知、可能性，以及想知道門後有什麼的渴望。「叩叩」成為核心的語言與視覺手法：我們嘗試、觸碰、敲門，然後等待回應。

幾何化的門構成系統的基本結構，白色多邊形標示門被敲響的位置；放射狀的形體與大面積的色塊，則把敲門時發出的聲音與能量視覺化。

設計週的活動類型很多，因此識別必須有系統又有彈性。同一組核心元素可以重新組合，應用在邀請卡、識別證、展覽圖像、Behance Reviews 與工作坊物料上，並維持一致的視覺語言。

Giving a knock a shape讓敲門聲有形狀

A geometric door and a white point of contact form the identity’s basic elements. In motion, radiating shapes and colour turn the energy of a knock into a visual sequence.幾何化的門與白色敲擊點，構成識別的基本元素。在動態中，放射的形體與色彩把敲門的能量轉成連續畫面。

Badges and objects識別證與活動物件

The door form becomes a faceted badge, while the same colour system carries into the Portfolio Reviews objects. A small set of elements connects the different activities within Design Week.門的形體轉化成多面向的識別證，同一套色彩也延伸到 Portfolio Reviews 的活動物件。少量的共同元素，連結起設計週裡不同類型的活動。

Portfolio Reviews in motionPortfolio Reviews 的動態延伸

The Portfolio Reviews animation reorganises the same geometric and colour elements for another part of the programme. The identity changes composition while retaining its connection to the wider event.Portfolio Reviews 動畫重新組合幾何與色彩元素，對應設計週中的另一項活動。構圖可以改變，與整體活動的關係仍然清楚。

Design Week film設計週影片

The event brings exhibitions, workshops, talks and portfolio reviews into one programme. The film provides a wider view of the setting in which the identity was used.展覽、工作坊、講座與作品集評論共同構成設計週。影片提供更完整的活動情境，呈現這套識別如何進入其中。
