Under Alien Skies在異星的天空下

Book cover書籍封面

Under Alien Skies is a space-travel guidebook imagined through awe, scale and the poetry of cosmic phenomena. The cover visualises a traveller’s perspective; the design positions the book as both a guide and an invitation to the sublime.

《在異星的天空下》是一本以敬畏、尺度與宇宙現象的詩意所想像的太空旅行指南。封面呈現旅人的視角，讓這本書既是指南，也是一封通往崇高之美的邀請。

The image as an object從圖像到書本

The mountain horizon gives the star field a traveller’s point of view. Seen at an angle, the cover, spine and reflective page edge bring that distant scene into the physical scale of a book.群山的地平線，讓星空有了旅人仰望的視角。從斜角觀看，封面、書脊與反光書口一起把遙遠的景象帶回一本書的實體尺度。
