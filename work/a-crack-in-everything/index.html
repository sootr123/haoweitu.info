A Crack in Everything萬物之隙

Book cover書籍封面

A Crack in Everything is a book cover design inspired by the paradox of the black hole: a boundary into which matter can fall but from which nothing can escape. Swirling linework visualises gravitational pull and the bending of space–time, while the composition appears to draw both image and typography towards a central void. The visual system turns an invisible astronomical force into a graphic experience of movement, distortion and depth.

The cover reflects the book’s theme, How Black Holes Came in from the Cold and Took Cosmic Centre Stage, by presenting the black hole not simply as an illustrated object but as a force that actively structures the page itself.

《萬物之隙》的封面設計，來自黑洞的悖論：一個物質可以落入、卻沒有任何東西能逃脫的邊界。旋轉的線條呈現引力的拉扯與時空的彎曲，整個構圖彷彿把圖像與文字一起吸向中央的空洞，讓一股看不見的天文力量，成為一種關於運動、扭曲與深度的視覺經驗。

封面呼應本書主題——黑洞如何從冷門題目走到宇宙舞台的中心——黑洞不只是被描繪的物件，而是一股主動決定版面結構的力量。

Type under gravity引力之中的文字

Fine lines curve towards a central void while the title stretches across the cover. The black hole becomes a force shaping the composition, drawing typography and image into the same movement.細線向中央的空洞彎曲，書名則延展至整個封面。黑洞成為組織版面的力量，讓文字與圖像一起被捲入相同的動勢。
