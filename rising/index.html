Selected work
for RISING為 RISING
精選的作品

Six projects connecting an idea across identity, print, motion and physical space. Selected for RISING's Senior Designer role, with a focus on cultural campaigns, adaptable visual systems and design direction.六件作品，呈現一個構想如何延伸到識別、印刷、動態與實體空間。針對 RISING Senior Designer 職缺，精選文化活動宣傳、可延展的視覺系統與設計指導案例。

01

A playful campaign idea carried from posters into an exhibition entrance.以鮮明的宣傳構想，串連系列海報與展覽入口。

2021

D&AD Exhibition in Taiwan 2021D&AD 設計展在台灣 2021↗

D&AD's familiar pencils open to reveal Taipei's fruit. The same idea connects poster imagery with an oversized entrance installation. Art / creative direction by Ken Tsai Lee; 3D modelling by Chiao Chang.把 D&AD 熟悉的鉛筆剖開，露出台北的水果，讓同一構想從海報延伸到入口的巨型裝置。藝術／創意總監為李根在，3D 建模為 Chiao Chang。

Role角色Designer / illustrator設計／插畫

02

One event identity adapted across programme streams, printed matter and animation.以一套活動識別，連結不同節目、印刷物與動畫。

2019

Taiwan Tech Design Week臺科大設計週↗

Geometric doors and radiating colour turn a knock into a flexible identity for exhibitions, talks and workshops. Shared elements connect invitations, badges, animated banners and Portfolio Reviews materials.以幾何門形與放射色塊，把敲門聲轉化為展覽、講座與工作坊的共同識別。相同元素重新組合於邀請卡、識別證、動態橫幅與作品集評閱物件。

Role角色Art / creative director, with the Design Week team藝術／創意總監，與設計週團隊合作

03

A Melbourne identity connecting signage, uniforms and a modular illustration system.為墨爾本品牌串連招牌、制服與模組化插畫系統。

Downtown Grocer好鄰居 Downtown Grocer↗

A smiling mascot and isometric shop illustration create a recognisable identity for a supermarket in Melbourne's Chinatown. Electric blue and neon green carry through the checkout, staff aprons, baskets and signage.以微笑角色與等角商店插畫，為墨爾本唐人街超市建立容易辨認的識別。電光藍與螢光綠延伸到收銀台、員工圍裙、購物籃與招牌。

Role角色Co-designer with Dawei Ding, D&D Creative與 D&D Creative 的 Dawei Ding 共同設計

04

Design direction for a consistent identity across packaging, workwear and site graphics.以設計指導建立一致識別，延伸到包裝、工作服與現場圖像。

HNZ Constructions↗

One geometric structure reads as H, N or Z through rotation and reflection. The identity remains recognisable across presentation packaging, rulers, workwear, helmets, signage and tape.一個幾何結構透過旋轉與鏡射，分別讀作 H、N、Z。識別延伸至展示包裝、尺、工作服、安全帽、指標與膠帶，在不同尺度維持辨識度。

Role角色Design director, with D&D Creative設計總監，與 D&D Creative 合作

05

A continuous visual story across portraits, animation and a proposed vehicle livery.以連續的視覺敘事，串起人物攝影、動畫與車身提案。

EndoAware↗

For a collaborative future-health concept at Design Factory Melbourne, a yellow awareness ribbon connects portraits, animation and a proposed screening bus. The line gives the identity a consistent narrative across formats.這是墨爾本設計工廠的未來健康協作概念。黃色關懷絲帶連結人物攝影、動畫與篩檢巴士提案，讓一條線在不同媒介中延續相同的視覺敘事。

Role角色Brand designer, CBI A³ student collaboration品牌設計，CBI A³ 學生協作專案

06

A festival identity study translating music and performance into fabric, imagery and print.藝術節識別研究，將音樂與表演轉化為布料、影像及印刷物。

2017

Taiwan International Festival of Arts臺灣國際藝術節↗

A student identity study with Jia Rong Liu, supervised by Ken Tsai Lee. A split-square pattern evokes piano keys and dance, moving between fabric, staged photography, publications and stationery.與劉佳蓉共同設計、由李根在指導的學生識別研究。分割方塊呼應琴鍵與舞姿，從布料與攝影延伸到出版物及事務用品。

Role角色Co-designer, student project共同設計，學生專案
