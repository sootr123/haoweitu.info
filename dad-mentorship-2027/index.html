D&AD New Blood Awards 2027
Mentorship ProgrammeD&AD New Blood Awards 2027
輔導計畫

A free online mentorship for university design students in Taiwan who want to take on a D&AD New Blood Awards 2027 brief. Weekly sessions run in Mandarin, with materials in English.為台灣熱愛設計的大學生與研究生而設的免費線上輔導，陪你挑戰 D&AD New Blood Awards 2027 的競賽題目。每週線上會議以中文進行，搭配英文資料。

Apply by 30 November 2026報名截止：2026 年 11 月 30 日

Apply by emailEmail 報名→See the 2027 briefs查看 2027 競賽題目↗

What you get計畫內容

  1. 01

    Design guidance專業設計指導

    Weekly feedback on your idea, process and craft, from first concept to final piece.每週針對概念、過程與執行細節給予回饋，從發想一路到完稿。

  2. 02

    Competition guidance競賽策略

    Choosing a brief, reading what it really asks for, and presenting the work so it reads clearly.挑選適合的題目、讀懂題目真正的要求，並讓作品的呈現清楚有力。

  3. 03

    Portfolio advice作品集建議

    Turning the project into a strong portfolio piece, with advice on your wider portfolio.把這次的作品整理成作品集的亮點，並給予整體作品集的建議。

Who can apply申請資格

  1. 01

    You are currently studying for a bachelor's or master's degree.目前就讀大學部或碩士班。

  2. 02

    You can join weekly online meetings from 15 December 2026, spend 1–2 hours a week on the project, and submit your entry before 11 March 2027.可以從 2026 年 12 月 15 日起每週參加線上會議，每週投入 1–2 小時，並在 2027 年 3 月 11 日前完成投件。

  3. 03

    You may apply as a team of two, as long as both members meet the criteria above.可兩人組隊報名，兩位成員都需符合上述資格。

How to apply報名方式

  1. 01

    Short cover letter簡短自我介紹信

    Who you are and why you want to join. Include your email, school, programme and year.你是誰、為什麼想參加。請附上 Email、學校、科系與年級。

  2. 02

    Simple CV簡易履歷

    One page is enough.一頁即可。

  3. 03

    Selected work作品選集

    Any format: a website, PDF or link.格式不限：網站、PDF 或連結皆可。
